Welcome to Crescent National Academy

Established at Chunauti Kuan, Khanquah More, Isapur Road, Phulwari Sharif, Patna, Crescent National Academy is a co-educational institution committed to academic brilliance and holistic personality development from Pre-Nursery through Class 10th.

We follow the CBSE curriculum pattern, prioritizing concept mastery over mechanical cramming. Our faculty members provide loving care to pre-primary learners, structured foundational concepts to primary students, and disciplined board-level coaching for secondary classes.

Complete Educational Spectrum

Academic Wings: Pre-Nur. to Class Xth

Structured pedagogical progression designed to foster foundational joy, intermediate curiosity, and board examination mastery.

Early Childhood • Pre-Nur, Nursery, LKG, UKG

Pre-Primary Wing

A warm, nurturing environment where toddlers take their first steps into learning through play, phonics, motor skill development, and creative expression.

Focus Areas:

Phonics, English & Hindi alphabet recognition, counting, fine motor control, coloring, rhymes, and social interaction.

Play-Way Methodology:

Montessori-inspired toy blocks, picture storytelling, tactile puzzles, and cheerful learning corners.

Child Care:

Dedicated female attendants, safe child-friendly furniture, and constant teacher supervision.

Special Admission:

Free registration and 3 months fee offer available for early pre-primary admissions!

Pre-Primary Highlights

  • Playful atmosphere with joyful learning materials
  • Gentle introduction to speaking and listening skills
  • Clean, hygienic surroundings & safe drinking water
  • Regular feedback sessions for parents
  • No heavy bags & no pressure learning
Foundational Stage • Classes 1, 2, 3, 4 & 5

Primary School Wing

Building fundamental literacy, numeracy, creative expression, and social skills through an engaging, activity-based CBSE curriculum.

Key Subjects:

English Language & Literature, Hindi, Mathematics, Environmental Studies (EVS), Basic Computer Awareness, Moral Values, Art & Craft.

Learning Focus:

Reading fluency, arithmetic confidence, curiosity about the natural world, neat handwriting, and team activities.

Textbook Policy:

Permanent, stable syllabus and book policy so textbooks aren't frequently changed every year.

School Timings:

8:00 AM – 1:30 PM (With designated snack recess and playground periods).

Primary Wing Highlights

  • Low student-teacher ratio for individualized attention
  • Spoken English and moral recitation practice
  • Regular parent-teacher progress evaluations
  • Handwriting and mental math drills
  • Co-curricular games and cultural participation
Preparatory Stage • Classes 6, 7 & 8

Middle School Wing

Middle school transitions students from basic concepts to abstract problem-solving, introducing distinct scientific and social science disciplines.

Key Subjects:

English, Hindi, Mathematics, Integrated Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Social Sciences (History, Civics, Geography), Urdu / Sanskrit, Computer Science.

Practical Science:

Hands-on experiments in composite science labs and hands-on computer typing/programming sessions.

Analytical Focus:

Deductive math reasoning, project-based assignments, debate competitions, and creative writing.

Special Benefits:

Free admission & 3 months fee waiver offer available. Contact 9507995880 for details.

Middle Wing Highlights

  • Science and Math Olympiad guidance
  • Interactive computer lab with Python/Scratch basics
  • Inter-house debate, elocution & essay writing
  • Remedial support classes for students needing extra care
  • Physical fitness and team sports coaching
Secondary Stage • Classes 9 & 10 (Board Years)

Secondary Wing (Class 9 & 10)

Result-oriented academic coaching geared towards CBSE pattern Board Examination excellence and solid foundation for future competitive exams.

Core Subjects:

English Language & Literature, Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Social Science, Hindi / Urdu, Information Technology (IT).

Board Exam Preparation:

Rigorous mock tests, previous 10-year question papers solving, answer framing techniques, and time management drills.

Practical Work:

Thorough laboratory manuals, viva voce preparation, and science experiment validations.

Career Counseling:

Stream selection guidance (Science, Commerce, Arts) and post-10th career roadmaps.

Secondary Wing Highlights

  • Consistent 100% board examination record
  • Extra doubt-clearing sessions after regular school hours
  • Chapter-wise question banks and continuous testing
  • Individual mentoring for high academic percentile
  • Direct interaction with senior experienced teachers
